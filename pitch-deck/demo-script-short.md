# Dead Man's Vault — Short Demo Script (~1 min)

## Target
Hackathon judges, Twitter/X clips, quick pitch.

---

### INTRO (0:00 - 0:10)

**[Show: App splash screen / Dashboard]**

> "Dead Man's Vault is an autonomous crypto inheritance protocol built for Solana Seeker. No backend, no intermediaries — everything runs on-device or on-chain."

---

### THE PROBLEM (0:10 - 0:18)

**[Show: Dashboard with wallet connected]**

> "Over $140 billion in crypto is locked in wallets with no recovery plan. If you lose access, your assets are gone forever. There's no 'next of kin' form in crypto."

---

### SETUP (0:18 - 0:35)

**[Show: Vault tab > Setup Wizard — quickly tap through]**

> "Setup takes 60 seconds. Add your beneficiaries, set their share percentages, choose your heartbeat interval — weekly, bi-weekly, monthly — and activate."

**[Show: EstateReview screen with cost breakdown — Vault Rent ~0.015, Agent Funding ~0.05]**

> "The vault creates a secure on-chain PDA, and funds an autonomous agent key in the device's TEE. Everything happens in a single transaction."

---

### HEARTBEAT + ESCALATION (0:35 - 0:48)

**[Show: Dashboard in demo mode — tap heartbeat, show Stage 0 green status]**

> "Just confirm a heartbeat periodically. One tap. If you stop responding, the protocol escalates through 4 warning stages over 17 days — notifications get more urgent at every stage."

**[Show: Notification examples on screen]**

> "If you never respond, Stage 4 triggers autonomously."

---

### EXECUTION (0:48 - 0:58)

**[Show: Execution logs screen with completed steps]**

> "The agent distributes SOL and tokens to each beneficiary on-chain, records the execution, refunds its own SOL, closes the vault, and destroys its key. Every step has a transaction signature you can verify on Solana Explorer."

---

### CLOSE (0:58 - 1:05)

**[Show: Website or pitch deck title slide]**

> "Dead Man's Vault. Your crypto should outlive you. Available now on devnet — download the APK at dmv.palatinearc.com."

---

## Tips
- Use demo mode (Settings > 5-tap version text) for fast escalation (30s per stage)
- Have a vault already set up before recording — setup wizard is the slow part
- Pre-fund the vault with some devnet SOL + a test SPL token for visual impact
- Keep energy high, speak clearly, cut dead air
